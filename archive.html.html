Zindagi Ka Har Shakhs

Zindagi ka har shakhs mohabbat nahin hota, Kuch log milte hain, par unki zarurat nahin hoti. Yeh chhin jaate hain kai raaston mein kahin, Unke baad bhi unki kami kabhi poori nahin hoti.

Rakh Ke To Dekh Wafa-E-Umeed

Rakh ke to dekh wafa-e-umeed parinde se bhi, Kya pata nikal jaye wafadaar koi is jahan mein.

Nahi Rehti Wafa Har Shakhs Mein

Nahi rehti wafa har shakhs mein Noor, Rishte, nazrein, dil badal jaate hain faqt kuch lafz ke khatir. Ek lafz ke khatir badal jaate hai wafadaar bhi Tum kya samajhte ho mohabbat hamesha qaim rehti hai

Nahi Hai Sunne Ko Koi Paband

Nahi hai sunne ko koi paband kisi ka, Sabka apna Khuda hai, sabka apna jahan hai.

Har Shakhs Ke Dard Ko Dard Samajhiye

Har shakhs ke dard ko dard samajhiye, janab, Yahan aap hi nahi hain bas qabil-e-dukh.

Ye Duniya, Ye Zindagi

Ye duniya, ye zindagi, Ye saazishen faqat ek lamhe ki maari hain.

Tu Dhoonde Zameen

Tu dhoonde zameen, main aashiyaan dhoondun. Tu kahe to main iss zameen par sitara dhoondun.

Kehne Ko Nazare

Kehne ko nazare bohot hain duniya mein, Lekin teri nazron sa nazara kahan milega.

Wo Ek Zakhm

Wo ek zakhm ek yaad mein hai, Khali pannon ki fariyaad mein hai. Utar chuka hai mere zehen se wo, Ab kisi aur raste ki aaghaaz mein hai.

Rehta Hai Faqat Ab Meri Shayari Mein Wo

Rehta hai faqat ab meri shayari mein wo, Meri yaadon mein uski kahi jagah nahin.

Noor-e-Jahan Aur Noor-e-Nazar

Noor-e-jahan aur Noor-e-nazar mein, Tasawwur bhi naa kar sake hum, ki kaun zyada haseen hai. 😶

Nazron Ka Taqaza

Nazron ka taqaza hai jhuk jaayen dekhein to na-mehram, Wo mehram ban ke mile to koi aur baat hogi.

Ek Sukoon Hai

Ek sukoon hai jo andar barpa sa hai, Ek khamoshi hai jo shor machana chahti hai.

Na Chahti Hoon Aaun Main

Na chahti hoon aaun main logon ki tawajjo mein, Chahti hoon ki phir koi to dekhe mujhe.

Koi Mile Jo Mujhse Hi Wabasta

Koi mile jo mujhse hi wabasta ho bas, Koi to aisa ho jiske liye duniya-o-zamana gawaya ja sake.

Koi To Ho Mujhse Wabasta

Koi to ho mujhse wabasta, Ki meri kisi shay se use bezaari na ho.

Dil Ke Dariya Mein

Dil ke dariya mein aik aawaz behti hai, Ki tu ruk jaa, aage saaz rehti hai. Wo kar degi zabt tujh pe, Phir naa laut paayegi tu kabhi zindagi mein.

Kuch Waqti Hālaton Mein

Kuch waqti hālaton mein, Kuch rakhi chhupi bāton mein, Badalna padhta hai... 😶

Mujhe Gawani Hai Duniya Apni

Mujhe gawani hai duniya apni, Mujhe chahna hai kisi shakhs ko toot kar. Lekin ye bezaari aur danish, Meri aqal mujhe amada nahi karti. 😶

Ye Duniya Ki Sargoshiyaan

Ye duniya ki sargoshiyaan, ye lehzon ka dukh, Kahan jaaun iss bezaar dil ko lekar?

Ye Saiyaara, Ye Duniya

Ye saiyaara, ye duniya, ye lehje, ye log, Aur inki kashmakash mein maari zindagi. Wah naseeb, tere bhi khel nirale. 😶

Kashmakash Mein Guzar Jaayegi Zindagi

Kashmakash mein guzar jaayegi zindagi, Do raah chal padoge jab tum, To kya tab wo dost yaad aayenge?

Ye Tanhaayi Aur Veerani

Ye tanhaayi aur veerani kisi kaam ki nahi, Jab faaslon ka daur ho, To kaun kis ke saath ho.

Koi Ulfat Si

Koi ulfat si, koi chaahat si, Koi mere dil ki aahat si khauf mein daal deti hai. 😶

Iss Lehje Ki Bezaari

Iss lehje ki bezaari ko kaun samjhega, Iss dil ki veerani ko kaun samjhega.

Kehte Hain Likha Hai Naseebon Mein

Kehte hain likha hai naseebon mein jo mil hi jaayega, Lekin mere naseeb, mera haath jab utha dua ke liye, To tere liye dua karne se khaali nahi gaya.

Main Jise Jaanti Bhi Nahi

Main jise jaanti bhi nahi, usi ka intezaar karti hoon. Kamaal ladki hoon, Khud hi chah kar, khud hi malaal karti hoon.

Tera Naam Abhi Lafzon Mein

Tera naam abhi lafzon mein aaya nahi, Magar dil pe pehra gehra hai. Main jaanti nahi tujh ko abhi, Magar ye dil abhi bhi tera hai.

Ya Rabb, Use Meri Tarah Tanha Na Rakhna

Ya Rabb, use meri tarah tanha na rakhna. Use mehfilon mein rakhna, Par meri kami uske dil mein zaroor rakhna.

Main Adhoori Hoon Abhi

Main adhoori hoon abhi, hamari mulaqaat baaqi hai. Teri meri zindagi ka saath baaqi hai. Dekhne hain abhi hame maslay hazaar saath. Mere zehen mein to nahi, magar dil mein, Tujhse milne ka qarar baaqi hai.

Ye Dil Na Sahi

Ye dil na sahi, zubaan samjho tum. Samajhna hai mujhko to aasman samjho tum. Nahi aaya samajh mein aasman, To suno— Mere dil ki hi zubaan samjho tum.

Na Dil Ho Tum

Na dil ho tum, na zubaan ho tum. Ek rooh ki alag hi daastaan ho tum.

Main Hoon Bhi Ek Dariya

Main hoon bhi ek dariya, Aur nahi bhi hoon ek qatra. Main hoon ek poori ghazal, Aur nahi hoon ek lafz bhi. Main hoon wajood se anjaan apne.

Kabhi Khamoshi Bhi Cheekhti Hai

Kabhi khamoshi bhi cheekhti hai bayan hone ke khatir, Kabhi shor bhi chuppi saadh leta hai khamosh ho jaane ke khatir.

Khud Hi Khud Ki Talaash

Khud hi khud ki talaash hoon. Khuda se main yun paas hoon. Na zameen mein hoon main, Na aasman mein hoon. Kahin beech mein atka jahan hoon. Har cheez meri mamuli hai, Har baat meri adhoori hai.

Main Yun Zindagani Mein

Main yun zindagani mein apni rooh ko talaashti hoon. Na kisi ko chhoone deti hoon rooh apni, Na khud rooh ke paas jaati hoon. Koi aaye milne mujhse, To kahun— "Ruko, abhi bula ke laati hoon."

Yun Duniyawi Lehjon Mein

Yun duniyawi lehjon mein khud ko kho baithi hoon, Khud hi apne logon se yun door ho baithi hoon.

Ehsaason Ko Zahir Na Hone Diya

Ehsaason ko zahir na hone diya, Khamoshi ko raaz hi rakha apni. Inteshaar mein bhi sukoon rehna seekh liya, Yeh hunar bhi kamaal ka hai zindagi ne diya.

Kabhi Khud Se Bhi Guftagu

Kabhi khud se bhi guftagu ka talluq na hota mera, Kabhi lagta hai qalb aazurda hai bohot. Kabhi lagta hai baat hi paas nahi mere, Main hoon khud ki hi parchhai, Aur khud ko hi main dekh nahi paati.

Zindagi Ne Kuch Lamhe

Zindagi ne kuch lamhe aise dikhaye, Sab kuch beeta, par dil ne bayan na kiya. Na aankhon mein ashk aaye, na zubaan hili, Na koi lafz, na koi qissa bayaan hua. Khamoshi ne le li jagah meri, Na main raha, na mere alfaaz rahe.

Jhooth Nahi, Yahan Sach Bikta Hai

Jhooth nahi, yahan sach bikta hai. Chehron par chehre sajaye phirte hain. Na rishton ki fikr, na talluq-e-junoon, Bas yahi hai yahan ka junoon. "Inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'oon..."

Na Paaya Samajh Hai Zindagi Kya?

Na paaya samajh hai zindagi kya? Har sawaal hai mutalliq mujh se ismein yahan. Hum guzarte hain jahan mein faqat is tarah se zindaganiyon ko, Waqt ke saath guzarna sikha dete hain pareshaniyon ko.

Na Main Ek Hoon, Na Main Hazaar

Na main ek hoon, na main hazaar, Apni hi daastaan mein hoon, apna hi yaar. Kabhi lagta hai main kuch bhi nahi, Kabhi lagta hai meri hi zindagi main hoon. Main khud hi khud ka sawaal hoon, Aur phir khud hi uska jawaab bhi.

Na Dil Samjhi

Na dil samjhi, Na jaan samjhi. Na lafzon ka bayan samjhi, Na baaton ka maqam samjhi. Na aankhon ki zubaan samjhi.

Kya Sar Utha Paungi Main?

Kya sar utha paungi main? Kya nazrein mila paungi main? Jab hoga kun uska, Shukar ada kar paungi main.

Mohabbat Hui, Fasana Hua

Mohabbat hui, fasana hua. Ishq hua, aashiqana hua. Dekh kar kho gaye hum. Unki aankhen muddaton baad dekhin, To paaya hamari nazron par koi aur deewana hua.

Mat Ho Haawi Mere Har Ik Andaaz Par

Mat ho haawi mere har ik andaaz par, jaana. Main jahan bhi rukh karun, Mujhe bas tu nazar aata hai.

Sirf Tu Hi Kyun Mila

Sirf tu hi kyun mila mere iss dil ko bhaane ko? Aur bhi to log hain na iss jahan mein.

Aaye Agar Baat Kisi Ki Izzat Par

Aaye agar baat kisi ki izzat par, To thehrane lage use beghairat. Aayi baat apne upar jab, Kehne lage—jhooth hai sab.

Siqve Na Kariye

Siqve na kariye is zindagi se, Yeh bhi to apne hi usool par chalti hai.

Koi Mile Mutalliq Mujh Ko

Koi mile mutalliq mujh ko, Duniya-e-zamana gawane ko raazi hoon main.

Ye Logon Ke Lehje

Ye logon ke lehje, dil aur bezaari dikh jaane ke hunar ke saath, Kahan jaaun main?

Kahin Door Se Koi Sada

Kahin door se koi sada si aati hai, Koi paas hai mere, Koi saath hai mere.

Jo Ho Tum Apne Mere

Jo ho tum apne mere, To theherna nahi mere liye. Na doob jaaun main lekar tumhe daryaa mein uss. Ya main kahun zubaan apne dil ki— Bacha lo mujhe bhi, Aur bach jaao khud bhi. Agar shaq hai ki doob jaaoge tum bhi, To jaao, chale jaao. Nahi rokungi main tumko kahin.

Dekhti Hai Wo Bacchi

Dekhti hai wo bacchi mujhko jab, To kehti hai— "Ab ki main tumhe nahi jaanti."

Sochti Hoon Main Ye Baaton Mein

Sochti hoon main ye baaton mein, Wo mera na ho saka faqat yaadon mein. Shayad wo tha hi nahi Mutalliq mujhse.

Humko Laazim Tha Tere Makaan Mein

Humko laazim tha tere makaan mein aana-jaana, Yun tujhko dekhe bagair saans kahan chalti hai.

Yun Be-Hayaai Ke Daur Mein

Yun be-hayaai ke daur mein hum bhi na gir jaayen, Yun kashmakash-e-zindagi mein rafta-rafta na fisal jaayen.

Zindagi Mein Mohabbat Ka Fasana

Zindagi mein mohabbat ka fasana nahi raha, Ye hum nahi rahe ya ye zamana nahi raha.

Kisi Rooh Ki Zindagi Mein

Kisi rooh ki zindagi mein faqat aarzoo hi hai, Yun to na kahen ke woh poori na ho sake.

Har Shakhs Ke Andar

Har shakhs ke andar dhoondha hai us shakhs ko, Jise main jaanti bhi nahi. Par kisi shakhs ke andar, Woh shakhs mujhe kabhi na mil saka.

Kya Kahen, Kaise Kahen

Kya kahen, kaise kahen... Zindagani mein ye justuju hi reh jaati hai. Waise to marne se pehle, Maut kai baar dastak de jaati hai.

Aashiqon Ki Chahat Mein

Aashiqon ki chahat mein khud ko barbaad na kar, Veerani, ulfat mein yun khud ko aabaad na kar.

Yeh Khwahishon Ki Aarzooon Mein

Yeh khwahishon ki aarzooon mein kahin kuch ghalat na maang loon main. Yeh khwahishon ki aarzooon mein kahin kuch ghalat na maang loon main. Isi liye kehta hoon, Ya Rabb, Tu apni marzi se mujhe de.

Yun To Reh Loongi Main

Yun to reh loongi main faqat tere baghair bhi, Yun to reh loongi main faqat tere baghair bhi, Par yeh na kahungi ke intezaar nahin hai tera.

Wasal-e-Khwaab

Wasal-e-khwaab jabse hua hai tujhse, Humko to neend se mohabbat hi ho gayi.

Faqat Alfaazon Ko Chunne Mein

Faqat alfaazon ko chunne mein kitne bhi maahir hon, Lafzon mein sadaqat na ho to achha nahi lagta.

Posheeda Gehraaiyon

Posheeda gehraaiyon ko jaanne wale sukoon mein nahi rehte. Agar shak hai koi, to mil len humse.

Kisi Shakhs Ke Posheeda-Pan

Kisi shakhs ke posheeda-pan ko dekh kar, Har shakhs ke posheeda-pan ko dekh kar humne jaana hai ye, Ke sab se na milna hi achha hai.

Lekin Agar Hum Na Milen

Lekin agar hum na milen kisi se bhi, To khud ke posheeda-pan ko kaise chhupa paayenge?

Ek Aarzu Hai Faqat Jeene Ki

Ek aarzu hai faqat jeene ki, Ek chahat hai kisi ke saath marne ki.

Ek Chahat Hai Khud Tak

Ek chahat hai khud tak baatein rakhne ki, Ek khwahish hai sab ko dard batane ki.

Kuch Zakhm Duaon Se Bhi Bharte Hain

Kuch zakhm duaon se bhi bharte hain, Yun to na, kai log sajdon pe marte hain.

Har Sach Ko Gawah Ki Zarurat

Har sach ko gawah ki zarurat nahin hoti, Har jhooth ko saza ki zarurat nahin hoti. Kuch log bikhar jaate hain gawahi ke baad bhi, Kuch log mar jaate hain gunahon ke saath hi.

Kuch Raaste Hain Manzil Se Zyada Azeez

Kuch raaste hain manzil se zyada azeez, Kuch duayein hain jo safar mein qubool ho jaati hain.

Har Dua Qubool Ho

Har dua qubool ho, yeh bhi rehmat nahin hoti, Har galti ke baad maafi zarurat nahin hoti.

Insaan Kho Jaata Hai

Insaan kho jaata hai, Par uski awaazein gum nahin hoti.

Jo Khoya Tha

Jo khoya tha woh paas tha hi nahin mere, Jo paaya tha woh maine khud hi chhod diya.

Khwaabon Ki Ziyaarat

Aaj aaye hain dafnane qabristan khwaab mein, Keh ke gaye hain, aayenge phir ziyaarat karne.

Nazar Milin

Nazar milin, yun nazar jhuki, Nazar nazar mein baat chali, Kehna na tum mujh ko kali, Ek baat pe main to phisal chali, Na nazrein milin, na nazar jhuki, Maine to bas khwaab ki baat buni.

Khwaabon Ko Dekhte Rehna

Khawabon ko dekhte rehna bhi accha nahi hota noor, Zindagi main waqt milne par so bhi jaana chahiye

Us Masoom Ka Bachpan

Us masoom ka bachpan dushwar ho gaya, Kam-umri hi mein usne makhlooq ke rukhsaaron par naqaab dekh liye. Woh us tah mein utar gaya, Jahaan se koi laut kar nahin aata.

Be-Hamraah Safar

Hujoom ke saath nikle the, koson door aa gaye, Mud kar dekha, to safar mein koi hamraah na tha.

Tark-e-Ta'alluq

Tark-e-ta'alluq ki ada ka kya sochein, Hum to woh hain ke aap ko dekhein, to khamosh ho baithte hain.

Baqaa Aur Fanaa

Maine apni baqaa ko fanaa kar diya, Par tune ek nigah-e-didaar bhi na bakhshi mujhe.

Sham'a Ki Khaak

Sham'a ki khaak hoon main, Zaahir na ho sakungi is zamaana-e-bad-dil mein.

Hijr Ki Baat

Tu kehta to main tere ishq mein fanaa ho jaati, Magar tum to hamare hijr ki baat par hi ad gaye.

Khizaan Ki Tanhaai

Khizaan ki tanhaai mein udaasi utar aayi, Zakhmon ne bhi khamoshi ka naqaab odh liya.

Wahm Ki Kaifiyat

Wahm ki kaifiyat mein fikr bhi shaamil thi zara, Us takhayyul ne mera raaz be-naqaab kar diya.

Khud-Kalaami

Is hijrat ke mausam mein khaali-pan to aam hai, Is bezaari ka kya karein, chalo khud-kalaami karte hain.

Aitbaar-e-Insaan

Aitbaar-e-insaan karte na to kya karte, Hum unpe marte na to kya karte, Nida ko Feshardan na karte to kya karte, Khamoshi se na marte to kya karte.

Khayalon Ki Riwayat

Ye khayalon ki riwayat bhi ajab hai jaana, Kahin bhi mil jaate hain Mahal-e Talâghi par.

Baatein Yaad Aati Hain

Is hayat-e-safar mein kuch ghamgeen si raatein yaad aati hain, Kuch na kaho to rakhi-chhupi baatein yaad aati hain, Aur tum kya hi kar loge izhaar-e-mohabbat karke, Jab saamne wale ko uske chaand ki baatein yaad aati hain.

Chaand Aur Zameen

izhaar-e-mohabbat kar ke faqat tumhe milna kya hai, Woh chaand hai aasmaan ka, aur tum ho zameen ke phool.

Daaghon Se Mohabbat

Yun daaghon se mohabbat kar ke dekho to sahi, Kitna tadapta hai faqat insaan doosre ke liye.

Hasr Ki Talaash

na safr e hayat hai, na mout mere paas hai, Mera hashr mujhse naraz hai, Us ghar ki mujhko talash hai, Na Hamsafar mera bana hamnawa, Iss dard main hu ab main jee raha,

Khud-Kalami Karte Karte Khud Ko Padhna Seekh Liya

Khud-kalami karte karte khud ko padhna seekh liya, Chahat ko peeche rakhke aage badhna seekh liya, Fitrat ko uski hamne samajhna seekh liya, Katron ko peche chode samandar tak badhna seekh liya, Pehni nahi khaal bhediye ki na bane ham bhed iske beech me jo hota hai wo hi banna seekh liya, Yum sheshe main bhi kya dekhen dikhta hai bss chera hi qubool karna seekh liya, Jo kaante lagte jaate kya darna hamko unse bhi jab jaani hain itni baate to inse bhi ladhna seekh liyaa, Unki na mafi par bhi maaf karna seekh liya, Safr e hayat main aage chalna seekh liya, Yadon ko manzil tak rakhna seekh liya, Sukoon ki talash main haqeqat se faasla karna seekh liya, Lagta to aisa hi hai shayad ye sab seekh liya?

Badal Gayi Hu

Badal gayi hu, Badal rahi hu, Badaln hi achha hai, Iss hayat-e-safr main na bikharna hi accha hai, Uthke phir se chalna hi accha hai, Khamoshi se milna hi accha hai, Dusron ko khne dena hi accha hai, Khud chup rehna hi accha hai, Khud sambhal kar aage badhna hi accha hai.

Har Rooh Ka Mausam

Har rooh ka mausam gumshuda hai kahin, Har rooh ki veerani mein awaaz nikalti hai.

Har Rooh Ka Mausam

Har rooh ka mausam gumshuda hai kahin, Har rooh ki veerani mein awaaz nikalti hai. Yun cheekhti hai faqat sunne ke khaatir woh, Ke sun le koi, mere dil se aah nikalti hai.

Faqat Chand

Faqt chand iss zamen par hota hai nahi, Ik chand hota jo kisi ka nahi hota....

Har Muskurahat Ke Peeche

Har muskurahat ke peeche sukoon nahin hota, Har aarzoo ke peeche justuju nahin hoti. Yun to mil jaate hain kai log zamaane mein, Par har shakhs ki hame zarurat nahin hoti. Yun aankhon ke ashk bhi puraane hi sahi, Har ashk ke peeche kahaani puraani nahin hoti. Yun dil mein behte hain dariya kai waise, Par har dariya ke bahaav mein rawani nahin hoti.

Dekhenge Khwaab Phir Se

Dekhenge khwaab phir se abhi waqt lagega. Dil ko sanwarne mein abhi waqt lagega, Ashkon ko girne mein abhi waqt lagega, Dehleez ko paar karne mein abhi waqt lagega, Doob jaane mein abhi waqt lagega, Bikhar jaane mein abhi waqt lagega, Khataayein bhulane mein abhi waqt lagega. Waqt lagega... Muddaton baad hui hai mulaqat khud se, To tujh ko bhulane mein abhi waqt lagega.
